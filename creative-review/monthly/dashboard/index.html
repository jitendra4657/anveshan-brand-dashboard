How this is built

Same rules as the weekly review. Types come from the ad name: Founder = KD or Aayushi videos · Expert = Poornima videos · Paid Partnership = Partnership_Ad_Macro/Micro/Nano · UGC = names with UGC · Product Video / Carousel = other Video and Cara_ ads · Static = Image_ · GIF = Gif_. Revenue and purchases use 1-day click; impressions, link clicks and video plays use Meta's default (video plays are wrong under 1-day click). Hook = 3-sec plays ÷ impressions · Thru-play = ThruPlays ÷ impressions · Hold = ThruPlays ÷ 3-sec plays. Concept status: New first spend this month · Scale ≥ 1.2× avg ROAS · Keep ≥ avg · Iterate 0.6–1× · Pause < 0.6× · Fatiguing ROAS down 30%+ MoM at ₹5k+ both months · Paused winner beat the average in an earlier month, now off · Watch under ₹5k this month.
