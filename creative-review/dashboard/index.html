How this is built

Types come from the ad-name convention: Founder = KD or Aayushi videos · Expert = Poornima videos · Paid Partnership = Partnership_Ad_Macro/Micro/Nano (Dr-named creators stay here) · UGC = names with UGC · Product Video / Carousel = other Video and Cara_ ads · Static = Image_ · GIF = Gif_. The hook / angle is the CamelCase phrase in the name (e.g. IsYourOilTruelyColdPressed). Meta revenue and purchases use 1-day click; impressions, link clicks and video plays use Meta's default. Verdicts: Scale ≥ 1.2× avg ROAS, Keep ≥ avg, Iterate ≥ 0.6× avg, Pause below that (all at ≥ ₹5k spend). Ads off-convention fall into Product / Static / GIF by format — name them by the convention to get them classified.
