How ad names are read

  • Names follow Format_[Creator]_Category_Product_[Pack]_Hook_Date, and partnership ads follow Partnership_Ad_Tier_Creator_Category_Product_[Pack]_Date. “– Copy” suffixes are ignored.
  • SKU: a product and a pack size are both named, e.g. GirDolchi_2.5L, GNO_15L, BMO15L, Khapli_5Kg.
  • Sub-category: a product is named without a size, e.g. Ghee_Halikar, Oil_GNO, Atta_HPA.
  • Category: Mix, a pack with no product (Ghee_Dolchi_2.5L), or two products of one category (BMO+YMO). Multi-category: Combo, Ghee+Oil, AllProduct.
  • Product codes: Gir, Halikar, Buffalo, DesiCow (breed not named) · GNO groundnut, SFO sunflower, BMO / YMO black / yellow mustard, BSME / BlackSesame, Almond, VCO, Coconut, Olive · Khapli, Multigrain, HPA / HP high-protein · Honey.
  • Only the ad name is read; ad set and campaign names are not used, even when they name a pack (e.g. BAU_ASC_Oil_15L).
